Organisation

Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF)

ASPF is an agriculture research and innovation organisation developing dependable technology for farms. Its work includes the AUDP protocol family, AUDP-ECM error correction, AUDP-DSP signal processing, and Nityaga RTOS for real-time agricultural control.

Principles

  • Mathematics and verifiable correctness where possible
  • Safety by design for field systems
  • Honest scope: real-time guarantees are not cryptography
  • Minimal public disclosure of internal methods
