Security model

Real-time guarantees are not security

Nityaga RTOS provides deterministic scheduling and timing bounds. It does not authenticate messages, prevent deliberate attacks, or replace cryptographic controls.

What it protects

  • Priority inversion and unbounded blocking
  • Deadline misses under defined load
  • Stack overflow via canaries and optional MPU
  • Unsafe actuator states on fault (fail-safe defaults)

What it does not protect

Deliberate modification of control messages, forged commands, or physical attacks on the device. Use authenticated encryption (AEAD) or a MAC on the application layer, with sequence numbers to prevent replay.

Reporting

Found a vulnerability? Report it privately through GitHub's Report a vulnerability button on the repository, or by email to sbruicesingh@haclon.in. Do not open a public issue.
