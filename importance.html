Why Real-Time Guarantees Are Required

  • Continuous unattended operation over long periods
  • Physical actions that cannot be easily reversed
  • Concurrent sensing, control, logging, and communication tasks
  • Remote and battery-powered field nodes
  • Safety and reliability take priority over general-purpose features

Scope: Nityaga RTOS provides timing and scheduling guarantees at the control layer. It does not replace application-level security, authentication, or higher-level farm management systems. Internal details remain confidential.
